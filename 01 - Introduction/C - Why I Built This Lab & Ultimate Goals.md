# Why I Built This Lab & Ultimate Goals

### The Catalyst: Developing Real-World Skills
As an entry-level professional aspiring to grow into a System Administrator role, I know it is easy to follow basic tutorials and click buttons in a Graphical User Interface (GUI). However, I wanted to build skills that actually translate to a real corporate production environment—where clicking buttons blindly doesn't scale and can cause widespread downtime. 

I built this lab to intentionally push past my comfort zone. My philosophy is that the best way to learn is to face real enterprise errors head-on, understand why things break, and master the foundational protocols (like DNS dependencies and Kerberos authentication) that keep businesses running. This project allowed me to step into the mindset of an Infrastructure Engineer, building a network from scratch with strict discipline from day one.

---

### My Professional & Personal Goals

* **Build a Disciplined Technical Foundation:** Prove that as an entry-level candidate, I understand the core concepts of transitioning standard Windows deployments into hardened, compliant corporate environments using strict Least Privilege models.
* **Adopt an Automation Mindset:** Train myself early in my career to recognize repetitive administrative tasks—such as employee onboarding—and learn how to safely use automation to eliminate human error and improve efficiency.
* **Perseverance Through Failure:** In IT, things break—just like in real-life situations. Because I am early in my journey, I encounter errors often, but I do not give up. This project is a testament to my resilience; when a configuration failed, I stayed disciplined, researched the logs, and worked through the problem until it was completely resolved.
* **Develop Methodical Troubleshooting Skills:** Learn to move away from guesswork. Documenting the 10 core case studies in this project trained me to approach problems analytically: identifying symptoms, finding the true root cause, and applying a permanent fix rather than a temporary band-aid.
* **Continuous Growth in the IT Field:** I am driven by a natural curiosity to explore new technologies and expand my skillset every single day. I am seeking an opportunity to bring this strong work ethic, adaptability, and proactive problem-solving attitude to an infrastructure team where I can learn, contribute, and grow.
