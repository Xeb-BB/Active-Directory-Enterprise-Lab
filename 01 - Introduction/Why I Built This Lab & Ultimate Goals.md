# Why I Built This Lab & Ultimate Goals

### The Catalyst: Moving Beyond the GUI
As a system administrator, it is easy to follow step-by-step tutorials to click buttons in a Graphical User Interface (GUI). However, in a real corporate production environment, clicking buttons doesn't scale, and minor misconfigurations can cause widespread downtime. 

I built this lab to intentionally break things, encounter real enterprise errors, and master the underlying protocols (like DNS dependencies and Kerberos authentication) that keep businesses running. It allowed me to step into the shoes of an Enterprise Infrastructure Engineer to design a network from scratch with security and scalability baked in from day one.

### My Professional Goals

1. **Master Enterprise Hardening:** Prove that I understand how to transition a standard "out-of-the-box" Windows deployment into a hardened, compliant corporate environment using strict Least Privilege models.
2. **Develop an Automation-First Mindset:** Train myself to look at any repetitive administrative task—such as onboarding an employee—and immediately seek to safely script it to eliminate human error.
3. **Refine Triage and Diagnostic Skills:** Cultivate a methodical approach to troubleshooting. Documenting the 10 core case studies in this project trained me to accurately identify symptoms, locate root causes, and apply permanent architectural fixes rather than temporary band-aids.
